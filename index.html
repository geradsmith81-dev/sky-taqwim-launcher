Sky Taqwim تقويم السماء

The Islamic calendar, Sunni and Shia, from NASA JPL astronomy: month starts, holy days, the Moon and the qibla.

Opening Sky Taqwim ...
Open Sky Taqwim
To put Sky Taqwim on this iPhone's home screen: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the top right on iPad or the bottom on iPhone), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

If it has been asleep it shows "This app has gone to sleep": tap the button there and wait about a minute.

Put it on your home screen

The page must be opened in Safari on iPhone/iPad, or in Chrome on Android.

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge): tap the three dots, then Add to home screen or Install app.

Then open it from the icon Sky Taqwim (a gold arch under the new crescent): it goes straight to the app.

The first time you tap Use my location in the app, allow location for the site. It is needed for sunset, prayer times and the qibla.

Free, no ads, no account. Sun and Moon from NASA JPL (DE440s ephemeris). Sunni and Shia practice are both given in their own terms. Your position is used only to calculate and is not stored.
